Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)

indb.js holds Indian recipes from the Indian Nutrient Databank, used with attribution:

  Vijayakumar A, Jaacks LM, et al. Development of an Indian Food Composition Database (Indian Nutrient Databank,
  INDB). Current Developments in Nutrition, 2024. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cdnut.2024.103790
  Data and code: https://github.com/lindsayjaacks/Indian-Nutrient-Databank-INDB-

The authors publish the data openly; the repository states no licence. INDB's values are computed from ICMR-NIN's
Indian Food Composition Tables 2017 and Nutritive Value of Indian Foods 2004, the UK McCance and Widdowson's
composition of foods integrated dataset, and USDA FoodData Central, with USDA nutrient retention factors. Recipes
come from The Art & Science of Cooking (Khanna et al., 2007), Basic Food Preparation (Raina et al., 2011) and
recipe websites.

What was changed for this app (test/build-indb.py): per 100 g energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fibre and free
sugars only; each recipe's own serving where it is one portion (10 to 500 g, not a jar); rows whose energy disagrees
with their own macros by over 30% removed; recipes over 35 g fat per 100 g removed (deep-frying oil counted as eaten),
except dressings.
